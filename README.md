### 💡 About Me

- 🏦 **What I do:** Senior Full-Stack Engineer focusing on high-availability backend systems with **C# .NET** & **SQL Server**, plus reactive frontend frontlines with **React** & **Vue.js**.
- 🛠️ **Core Philosophy:** Clean Architecture, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), solid unit testing, and building scalable software for high-demand environments.
- 🎓 **Background:** Started my journey studying Information Systems at UNNE (Argentina) 🇦🇷, with a background in civil engineering 🏗️.
- 💼 **Experience:** SaaS platforms, enterprise systems, and core banking applications.
- 🎯 **Current Focus:** Elevating enterprise frontend workflows, exploring modern cloud-native architectures, and automating dev workflows.
